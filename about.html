About UMS Groups

Engineers who stayed on site.

UMS Groups started as a two-person structural design practice. We kept the same standard as we grew into a full engineering and construction firm: nobody signs off on a drawing they wouldn't stand under.

How we got here

UMS Groups was founded by structural engineers who were tired of watching good designs get compromised on site by contractors chasing schedule. So we built our own construction and fabrication teams to work alongside the design office — the same people who size a beam are in the room when it's time to decide how it actually gets erected.

Almost two decades later, that's still the model. We take on structural engineering, civil construction and industrial fabrication work for clients who need a single firm to own the outcome, not just a set of stamped drawings.
