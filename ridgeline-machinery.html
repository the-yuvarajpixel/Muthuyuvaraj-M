Built by the people who use it

Every welder, mechanic, and machinist on our floor has run equipment in the field before they ever built it. That's not a hiring policy — it's just who was left when the last recession thinned everyone else out.

Ridgeline started in a single quonset hut outside Granite Falls, fixing combines for neighboring farms during harvest. When the equipment got bigger, so did we — but we never stopped hiring people who'd already spent a decade in a cab or under a machine.

Today our floor runs forty-two people across fabrication, hydraulics, electrical, and field service. Most came from operating the exact equipment they now build. That's the whole business model: nobody designs a fix for a problem they haven't personally cursed at.
