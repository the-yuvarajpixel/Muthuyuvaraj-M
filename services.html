Services

Four disciplines, one accountable team.

We scope these separately because clients hire us for different combinations of them — but every project draws on all four working together.

S-01

Structural engineering

Design and analysis for steel and reinforced-concrete structures — from concept through construction-issued drawings. We size, detail and certify the structure, and stay involved as the engineer of record through construction.

Includes

Load analysis · Foundation design · Steel & RCC detailing · Seismic & wind assessment · Peer review & certification

S-02

Civil construction

Site works, earthworks, foundations and heavy civil construction, built by our own crews rather than sub-contracted out. That keeps quality and schedule under one chain of command from groundbreaking to handover.

Includes

Site grading & earthworks · Foundations & footings · Retaining structures · Drainage & utilities · Concrete works

S-03

Industrial fabrication

In-house steel fabrication for structural frames, platforms, walkways and process support steelwork, produced to the same drawings our engineers issue — no tolerance lost in translation between design and shop.

Includes

Structural steel frames · Platforms & walkways · Pipe supports · Site erection · Protective coating

S-04

Project management

A single point of contact who owns cost, schedule and quality across every phase — so you get one report and one number to call, whichever discipline is active that week.

Includes

Scheduling & cost control · Procurement · Contractor coordination · Weekly progress reporting · Close-out documentation
